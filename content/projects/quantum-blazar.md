Quantum Blazar is a market research dashboard that brings stock and crypto scanning, news context, historical analysis, and rule-based paper trading into one workspace.

I built it to explore how market data, research tools, and automated workflows can work together—and to make the reasoning behind simulated trading decisions easier to inspect.

The project includes:

- Stage 2 market scanning and context leadership views.
- News-impact monitoring alongside market signals.
- Historical backtesting and a configurable paper-trading engine.
- A Python and FastAPI backend with a browser-based dashboard.

This is a research and paper-trading project. Any portfolio figures shown in the dashboard relate to simulation, rather than a claim of live investment performance.
