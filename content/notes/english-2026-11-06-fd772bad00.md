# 2026-11-06 Practiced natural English expressions

## Corrected sentences

| Natural English | 廣東話解釋 |
| --- | --- |
| I hope they have an update today. | 用 **an update**，唔係 **any update**（肯定句一般用 an）。 |
| If the information is correct, the plan should start soon. | **If + present simple**, 後面可以用 **should + verb** 表達預期。 |
| If we don't hear anything this week, they might update us next week. | **might** 表示有可能，但唔確定。 |
| I'm almost at the parking lot. | **almost at** 表示差唔多到達。 |
| There are a lot of people crossing the road. | **There are** 配合複數 people；**crossing** 描述正在過路。 |
| It's a little dangerous to drive too fast here. | **It's + adjective + to + verb**，常用嚟描述行為。 |
| Could you say that again in Cantonese? | 禮貌要求對方用廣東話重複。 |

## Vocabulary
- **update**：最新消息／更新
- **schedule**：時間表
- **survey**：問卷調查
- **delay / delayed**：延遲
- **pedestrian**：行人
- **cross the road**：過馬路
- **parking lot**：停車場
- **forecast**：天氣預報
- **chilly**：有啲凍
- **breezy**：有微風／較大風

## Speaking tips
- 想講「希望有消息」，可以用 **I hope there's an update**。
- 避免一句入面重複太多 **you know**；停一停再講下一個完整句子會更自然。
- 問天氣可以講 **What's the weather forecast for tomorrow morning?**。
