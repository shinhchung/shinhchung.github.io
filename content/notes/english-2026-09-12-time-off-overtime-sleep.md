## English practice — 補假、加班與睡眠

- 「補假」可按語境講：
  - `make-up day`：口語、易明，但唔一定係最正式 HR 用語。
  - `day in lieu` / `comp day`：較接近「補假／補休」。
  - `substitute holiday`：如果係用另一日代替原本假期。

- 今日練習句：
  - `Yesterday was a make-up day.`
  - `Most people didn't come into work because they prefer time off to extra pay.`
  - `Only a few of us showed up.`

- 較自然講「最近成日發惡夢」：
  - `I've been having a lot of bad dreams lately.`
  - `Why do you think I've been having so many bad dreams lately?`

- 睡眠表達可改自然啲：
  - 與其講 `I dreaming every time`，可講 `I've been dreaming a lot lately.`
  - 如果想表達惡夢令自己較易醒，可以講：`One small benefit is that it's easier for me to wake up after a bad dream.`
