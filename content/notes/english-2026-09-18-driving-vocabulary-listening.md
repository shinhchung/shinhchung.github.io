# 2026-09-18 — Commute English: driving & vocabulary

## Morning commute English practice

### Driving
- 今日遇到一次差啲追尾嘅情況：前車突然停低，因為車速唔快，所以及時煞停；後車亦及時停低。
- Useful corrections:
  - **The car in front of me suddenly stopped.**
  - **Luckily, I wasn't driving very fast, so I was able to brake in time.**
  - **The car behind me was going very fast and almost hit the back of my car.**
  - **If someone is driving aggressively, I'll just let them pass and move into the slower lane.**

### Vocabulary
- **矛盾** → **contradiction**
  - **There's a contradiction: I want to take public transit, but I also want to save time.**
- **公共交通工具** → **public transit / public transportation**
  - **public transit** 較簡潔自然。
- **不幸中之大幸 / 因禍得福** → **a blessing in disguise**
  - 指一件事起初睇落係壞事，但最後帶來好結果。

### Listening practice
聽咗一個短故事：有人落樓拎已送到嘅 package，忘記帶鎖匙，被鎖喺門外約 45 分鐘；鄰居最後幫佢入返去，佢送 cookie 多謝對方，之後兩人成為朋友。

理解重點：
- **pick up a package** = 去拎/領取包裹
- **get locked out** = 因冇鎖匙等原因被鎖喺門外
- 最後能夠完整重述故事內容。
