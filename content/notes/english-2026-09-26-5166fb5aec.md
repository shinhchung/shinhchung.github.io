# 2026-09-26 Drove safely through strong wind

## Short story

This morning, the wind was loud and the rain came down hard.

Derek sat in his car, hands on the wheel, taking a deep breath.

He said to himself:

> Slow is fine. Safe is better than fast.

He turned on a calm playlist, left extra space on the road, and kept telling himself:

> Arriving is the goal.

When he got to work, he was a little later than usual, but he was calm and safe — and that's what mattered.

## Useful weather vocabulary

- **high wind** — 強風
- **wind gust** — 陣風
- **heavy rain** — 大雨
- **coastal flooding** — 沿岸水浸
- **strange noise** — 奇怪／不尋常嘅聲音

## Useful English sentences

- **I want to learn more weather vocabulary, especially for weather like today.**
- **I heard a strange noise coming from my car. I'm not sure what it is.**
- **I heard a strange noise, but I think my car is fine.**
- **It was probably just the sound of the wind.**
