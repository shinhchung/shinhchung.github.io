# 2026-09-19

## Indoor bike mat / PLENY folding bike

- Bike: PLENY 5-in-1 Folding Exercise Bike.
- For this relatively light folding magnetic exercise bike, a 3 mm high-density mat is generally enough for:
  - floor protection
  - anti-slip
  - sweat protection
  - light vibration reduction
- If using it on the 2nd floor and worried about vibration/noise downstairs, add rubber anti-vibration pads under the bike feet. This is more effective than relying only on a thicker full-size mat.
- No strong need to replace a 3 mm mat with a 10 mm mat for normal seated cycling.

## English practice — Funny story

### Story summary

A woman went outside her apartment/building to pick up a package, but she forgot to bring her key. She got locked outside and had to wait for someone to come back into the building. After about 40 minutes, a neighbor arrived and helped her get back in. She felt embarrassed, so later she gave the neighbor some cookies. They eventually became friends.

### Useful corrected sentences

- She went outside to pick up a package.
- She forgot to bring her key.
- She got locked outside.
- She waited for about 40 minutes.
- Her neighbor came back to the building and helped her get in.
- She felt embarrassed.
- She gave her neighbor some cookies.
- They became friends.

### Vocabulary

- **pick up a package** = 拎／領取包裹
- **get locked outside** = 被鎖喺門外
- **neighbor** = 鄰居
- **embarrassed** = 尷尬
- **eventually** = 最後／後來
