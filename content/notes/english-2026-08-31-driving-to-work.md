# 2026-08-31

## English practice — driving to work

### Useful sentences

- **This bike actually follows the rules and doesn't cut the line.**
  - `follow` → third-person singular should be **follows**.
  - **follow the rules** = 守交通規則。
  - **cut the line** = 插隊；開車情境亦可講 **cut in front of someone**。

- **The bridge is closed, so I had to take a different route.**
  - **route** 比 `road` 更適合表示「路線」。
  - **had to** = 因為情況而「要／不得不」改路。

- **Now I'm back on my regular route.**
  - **regular route** = 平時慣常行嘅路線。

- **It's not a big deal.**
  - 表示「其實冇乜大不了／算啦」。

- **It's not worth getting angry. I just focus on driving safe.**
  - **It's not worth + -ing** = 唔值得為某件事而做某動作。
  - 更標準寫法可用 **I just focus on driving safely.**

### Driving discussion

- 路上每日都可能遇到亂切線、唔守規矩嘅司機；如果每次都嬲，基本上日日揸車都會嬲。
- 重點唔係享受返工車程，而係安全到達目的地：慢少少通常唔係大問題。
- Practical mindset: **Don't let other drivers make you angry. Focus on getting there safely.**
