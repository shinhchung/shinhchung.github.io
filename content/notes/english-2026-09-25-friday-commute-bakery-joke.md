# 2026-09-25 Practiced Friday commute English and a bakery joke

## Commute English

- **I'm driving to the company.**  
  更自然：**I'm driving to work.** / **I'm on my way to work.**
- **There's a little bit of traffic, but I don't think there's an accident. There are just a lot of cars on the road.**
- **Friday is usually a little busy, but on Saturday and Sunday there's almost no traffic.**
- **My estimated arrival time is 6:39. That's only a little later than usual.**
- **Don't rush. Take it easy.**
- **If public transportation were cheaper and faster, I would consider taking it to work. But right now, I still prefer driving.**

## Useful vocabulary

- **determined** — 已經下定決心、唔容易放棄  
  - *I'm determined to improve my English.*
- **out of service** — 暫停服務／唔使用得
- **passenger** — 乘客
- **frustrating** — 令人煩躁、挫敗

## Funny story — Day-old bread

A guy walks into a bakery and asks:

> “Do you have any day-old bread?”

The baker says:

> “Yes.”

The guy replies:

> “Great. I'll take some tomorrow.”

### Why it's funny

「day-old bread」即係放咗一日嘅麵包。個男人明明今日已經可以買到 day-old bread，但佢話「聽日先嚟買」，咁今日啲麵包到聽日又會變成 day-old bread。個笑位係佢將件簡單嘅事搞到好似特登要等麵包變舊。

實際上真係有人會買 day-old bread，因為通常會平啲，而且可以用嚟整 toast、breadcrumbs 等。

## Speaking practice

- **I don't want that to happen to me.**
- **It's funny but frustrating.**
- **I know what “out of service” means, but I didn't know “determined.”**
- **Determined means you've made up your mind and you're not going to give up.**
