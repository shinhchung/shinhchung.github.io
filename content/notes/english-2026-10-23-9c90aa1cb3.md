# 2026-10-23 Practiced PTO planning and natural English

## PTO 計劃
- 現有 PTO 約 147 hours，另有 floating holiday 24 hours；據目前理解，最多 carry over 120 hours。
- 預計年底前需要用 51 hours（147 - 120 + 24）；須向 HR 確認 floating holiday 到期及 carryover 規則。
- 每個工作日 12-hour shift，其中 1-hour unpaid lunch，請一日假需要 11 PTO hours。
- 請 5 日 = 55 hours，較 51 hours 多 4 hours。
- 平時 Friday–Sunday 工作；偏好 Friday 避開交通，或者 Sunday 配合朋友活動；不喜歡只請 Saturday，因為要 Friday、Sunday 分開返工。
- 初步考慮 November 請部分 Friday，December 保留日子與朋友聚會。
- Christmas Day 2026-12-25 是 Friday。若公司按政策給該日有薪假，考慮再請 December 26 (Saturday) 和 27 (Sunday)，共 22 PTO hours；需要 manager approval，亦要核實假期安排。
- 打算盡快提交 PTO request，尤其聖誕節熱門日期。

## Useful American English
| Natural expression | 廣東話意思 |
|---|---|
| I still have about five days of PTO I need to use before the end of the year. | 年底前仲有約五日假要用。 |
| I'm thinking about taking one day off each week or using all five days at once. | 考慮每星期請一日，定一次過請晒。 |
| My company only lets us carry over 120 hours of PTO. | 公司只准帶 120 小時 PTO 去下一年。 |
| I need to get my manager's approval before I can take time off. | 請假之前要經理批准。 |
| I should put in my PTO request as soon as possible. | 應該盡快交請假申請。 |
| I don't have any plans yet, but I want to keep my options open. | 暫時冇安排，但想保留彈性。 |
| There's a good chance my friends will plan something in December. | 朋友好可能十二月會約活動。 |
| Sunday would work better for me. | 星期日對我更合適。 |
| If I want to extend my Christmas break, I could take Saturday and Sunday off, too. | 想延長聖誕假，可以連星期六、日都請。 |
| Maybe I'll put in my PTO request tonight. | 可能今晚提交請假申請。 |

## Driving and music vocabulary
- **traffic jam**：塞車；Friday traffic can be heavy.
- **smooth jazz / gentle piano / instrumental music**：柔和爵士／輕鋼琴／純音樂。
- **relaxing but not too sleepy**：放鬆但唔會令人眼瞓。
- **Can you play some background music while we chat? Just instrumental music, no vocals.**：可唔可以傾偈時播背景音樂？只要純音樂，唔要人聲。
- Voice conversation 目前無法直接播放持續背景音樂；可以透過 Siri 啟動音樂 app，但音訊可能被 voice chat 暫停或降低音量。
