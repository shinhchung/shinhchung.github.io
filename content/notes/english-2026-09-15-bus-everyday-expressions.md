# 2026-09-15

## English practice + CCNA / commute notes

- CCNA progress: 最大問題係「難開始」同「開始後容易分心」，常見 distraction 包括 YouTube、彈 piano、食嘢。
- 建議用極細步開始：只做 5 分鐘，開 material、skim 一小段，完成就停，重點係降低開始阻力。
- Housing: 想開始了解買屋，即使而家 salary 未必夠高，都可以先研究可行性。
- Bus vocabulary:
  - `vehicle number` = 車輛編號；例如巴士車身上的 `6016` 多數係 vehicle number，唔一定係 route number。
  - `The bus in front of me.` = 我前面嗰架巴士。
  - `I saw a bus with the number 6016. Is that the route number, or just the bus number?`
- Public transit: 想研究由屋企去 Secaucus 公司嘅 bus / train 路線、車費同通勤時間。
- FedEx / iPhone return:
  - 如果 Apple 提供 prepaid FedEx label，一般可以放入 FedEx drop box。
  - 但 iPhone 價值較高，較穩陣做法係去 staffed FedEx location，攞 drop-off receipt 作證明。
  - Natural phrase: `drop it off in the FedEx drop box`。

### Useful English corrections

- `I should be doing more, but I didn't.`
- `It's hard for me to start, and it's hard to stay focused.`
- `There are so many things that distract me.`
- `I'm trying to find a new house.`
- `I don't think my salary is high enough right now, but I still want to try.`
