## Driving English Practice — Rain & Road Conditions

### Key vocabulary
- **puddle**：水氹；細至一般路面積水都可以用。
- **deep puddle**：較深嘅水氹。
- **flood / flooding**：水浸；當成條路或一大片地方都有積水，比 `puddle` 嚴重。
- **splash**：濺起水花。
- **skid**：車輛打滑、失去抓地力。

### Corrected sentence
> When there are deep puddles on the road, I slow down because the car might splash or skid.

較自然口語亦可以講：
> When I see deep puddles on the road, I slow down because the car might skid.

### Usage notes
- `When there has deep puddle` ❌
- `When there are deep puddles` ✅
- `When there’s a deep puddle` ✅（單數）
- `When there are floods everywhere` 文法上可以，但日常講路面情況更自然係：`There’s flooding everywhere.`

### Spelling
- **skid** = S-K-I-D
