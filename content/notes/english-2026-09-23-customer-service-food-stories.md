# 2026-09-23 Practiced customer service and food ordering English

## Customer service: canceled order and price increase

Scenario: an order was placed earlier, then canceled after the price increased by about $400.

Useful sentences:
- "Hi, I placed an order in June, but it was canceled. Now the same item costs $400 more. Is there any way to honor the original price or offer a credit?"
- "I understand policies can change, but this feels unfair. Is there anything you can do to help me today?"
- "My order was canceled after the price went up. Can you reinstate my order at the original price?"

Key vocabulary:
- **reinstate** = restore / put something back（恢復原本訂單）
- **honor the original price** = 按原本價錢處理
- **credit** = 店舖提供嘅金額補償／credit
- **the price went up** = 價錢升咗
- **canceled** = 被取消

Corrections:
- "My order get canceled." → **"My order was canceled."**
- "The price get increasing." → **"The price went up."** / **"The price increased."**
- "This feel unfair." → **"This feels unfair."**
- "Offer an credit." → **"Offer a credit."**

## Funny story 1: The giant cake

A guy was at a grocery store buying a **giant cake**. The cashier looked at the cake and asked, "Oh, big party?"

He replied, "No, it's just for me."

Everyone waiting in line suddenly got quiet. After a short pause, he said, "Just kidding. It's for my mom's birthday."

Everyone laughed.

Vocabulary:
- **giant** = very large
- **cashier** = 收銀員
- **in line** = 排隊中
- **pause** = 停一停
- **just kidding** = 講笑啫 / I'm joking
- **wink** = 眨一隻眼，通常表示講笑或暗示

## Funny story 2: The cat-food lunch mix-up

A woman decided to **meal prep**. She spent Sunday cooking food, putting it into plastic containers, labeling them, and stacking them in the fridge.

On Monday morning, she grabbed a container without looking carefully. At lunchtime in the office kitchen, she proudly opened the lid and discovered that she had packed her **cat's food by mistake**.

She did **not** eat the cat food. She just stared at it, sighed, and joked:

> "Well, at least the cat eats better than I do."

The funny part is that she accidentally grabbed the wrong container.

Details discussed:
- **container** here means a plastic food container, **not a can / 罐頭**.
- The cat food had been put into a similar container, so she mixed them up.
- The story does not establish whether the cat later ate her lunch.

Vocabulary:
- **meal prep** = 預先準備幾餐食物
- **label** = 貼標籤 / 標示
- **stack** = 疊起
- **container** = 容器；呢度係膠食物盒
- **grab** = 隨手攞
- **peel back the lid** = 打開／揭開個蓋
- **by mistake** = 不小心、誤咗
- **mix up** = 搞亂／攞錯
- **sigh** = 嘆氣

## Ordering pizza and fast food

Useful pizza order:
- **"Can I get one slice of pepperoni, please?"**
- **"Can I get one slice of this, please?"** — 唔知 pizza 名時可以直接指住。
- **"Can I get one slice of pepperoni and a six-piece order of Buffalo wings, please?"**

Pizza vocabulary:
- **cheese pizza** = cheese + tomato sauce，冇其他 topping
- **pepperoni pizza** = 上面有圓形薄片 pepperoni，通常有少少辣
- **sausage pizza**
- **mushroom pizza**
- **veggie pizza**
- **Hawaiian pizza** = ham + pineapple
- **Supreme pizza** = 通常有多種 meat + vegetables
- **slice** = 一件 pizza
- **topping** = pizza 上面嘅配料

Correction:
- "Six piece of Buffalo wing." → **"A six-piece order of Buffalo wings."**
- **wing → wings** when ordering several.

## Ordering at Popeyes / asking about sides

Useful sentence:
- **"What sides do you have?"**
- "I'll have fries, please."

Sides discussed:
- fries
- mashed potatoes and gravy
- red beans and rice
- coleslaw
- biscuit

Correction:
- "What side do you have?" → **"What sides do you have?"**

## Quick speaking pattern

A natural full order:
> "Can I get one slice of pepperoni and a six-piece order of Buffalo wings, please?"

If the employee confirms the order:
> "Yes, that's right."
