# 2026-11-10 Practiced explaining work changes in English

## Useful corrected sentences

- **I'm interested in the new schedule because it guarantees forty hours a week.**
- **I'm concerned about when the new schedule will start.**
- **I'm interested in the specialist role, but I'm worried about the pay and hours.**
- **I'm still studying for my CCNA, so I'm not sure if I'm qualified for the specialist role.**
- **I think the CCNA is a plus, but maybe not necessary.**
- **I'm curious about my English level, especially my ability to organize my thoughts. Do you think native English speakers can understand me?**
- **When I need to explain a complicated task, I break it down into simpler sentences.**

## Vocabulary and grammar

| English | 中文解釋 | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| interested | 有興趣（形容詞） | I'm interested **in** the role. |
| interesting | 有趣、令人感興趣（形容詞） | The project sounds interesting. |
| interest | 興趣（名詞）；令某人感興趣（動詞） | This job interests me. |
| worried | 擔心（形容詞） | I'm worried **about** the hours. |
| concerned | 關注、擔心 | I'm concerned about the start date. |
| qualified | 具備資格／能力 | Am I qualified for the position? |
| a plus | 加分項、優勢 | A CCNA would be a plus. |
| necessary | 必須、必要 | It may not be necessary. |
| organize my thoughts | 組織思路 | I need time to organize my thoughts. |
| break down | 拆細、分解 | Break down a complicated task. |
| especially | 尤其是、特別係 | Especially my speaking skills. |

## Pronunciation

- **especially** — /ɪˈspɛʃəli/，約讀 *ih-SPESH-uh-lee*；重音喺 **SPESH**，尾字母係 **-LY**。
- **interested** — 注意尾音 **-ed**，唔好講成 *I'm interest*。
- **José** — 西班牙文人名，約讀 *ho-SEH*。

## Speaking practice

- 用簡單完整句子講清楚一個意思，再接下一句。
- 練習 **I'm interested in...**、**I'm worried about...**、**I'm concerned about...**。
- 講複雜工作流程時可以 **break it down into simpler sentences**。
- 本次對話主要練習解釋輪班變動、GPU testing、specialist role，以及個人英語表達能力。
